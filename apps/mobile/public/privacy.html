Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Who we are

Our Players is made by Adveis, an individual developer based in Latvia. Contact: ourplayers@adveis.dev.

What the app is

Our Players shows how players from the countries you choose did in their games. There is no account, there are no ads, and there are no analytics or tracking services in the app. The only record is the server log described below.

On your device

The countries and leagues you follow, your favorites and your hidden players are stored on your device. Android's automatic backup may copy them to your Google account. Uninstalling the app removes them from the device.

Sent to our server

To fetch the right results, the app sends those preferences and any search text in the web address of each request, over an encrypted HTTPS connection. They are used to answer that request and then discarded. They are never saved in a database, and the request log leaves them out. The one exception is the database error described under "What's logged".

Your IP address

Your network address reaches our server, as with any internet request. It is held only in the server's memory, to limit abusive traffic, and is dropped by the first request after each minute, so it can stay longer when nobody else is using the app. It isn't logged or used to work out your location.

What's logged

For each request, the server logs the path (which may name a date, game or player), the status and how long it took. It logs no IP address and leaves out the rest of the web address, so a line can't be tied to a person. If one of our database lookups fails, the error line can also hold the values of that lookup, which can be your search text or the preferences above, still with no IP address. The proxy's error log, written when the API can't answer, holds the path and request headers such as the app or browser type. Logs are capped at 50 MB per service and overwritten as they fill, with no fixed time limit.

Deletion

There is no account and nothing tied to a person, so there is nothing we could find and delete on request. You can clear your data by uninstalling the app, or by clearing site data on the website.

Sharing

Nothing is sold or shared. The API runs on our rented server at Linode in Germany, which acts for us. The website version is served by Cloudflare, which handles visitors' addresses as any web host does. The Android app doesn't use Cloudflare for its data.

Data sources

Player and game data comes from public NHL and ESPN web APIs, fetched by our server on a schedule. No user data goes to them. Our Players is not affiliated with the NHL, NBA or ESPN.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13. It asks for no personal information and has no ads or accounts.

Your rights and changes

Write to the contact above with any questions. You may also complain to a data protection authority. Changes to this policy appear on this page with a new date.
